How to Choose a Desk Lamp for a Home Office (2026)

The problem: The desk is too dim, the light glares off the screen, or the lamp takes up the space you need.

How to use this guide

Criteria only: no products are named and no links are included. Check each point against your own space and the product page before you buy.

Buying criteria

  1. Brightness: check the stated lumens and whether it is dimmable; a fixed bright light can be harsh for long sessions.
  2. Colour temperature: check the stated kelvin range; adjustable warm-to-cool light lets you match the time of day and the room.
  3. Glare: check whether the light is shaded or diffused and can be aimed away from your screen and eyes.
  4. Reach and coverage: measure your desk depth and width and compare with the lamp's arm length and head angle.
  5. Footprint and mounting: base, clamp or screen mount, depending on how much desk surface you can give up.
  6. Power: check plug versus USB power, cord length and where your nearest outlet is.
  7. Controls: check where the switch or dimmer sits and whether you can reach it without stretching.

FAQ

Where should the lamp sit?

Many people place it on the side opposite their writing hand so the hand does not cast a shadow on the page. Then aim it so it does not reflect off the screen.

Is a monitor light bar enough on its own?

It lights the area in front of the screen. If you also read or write further across the desk, check whether its coverage reaches that area.

Warm or cool light?

It is personal preference. An adjustable colour temperature range avoids having to choose up front.
